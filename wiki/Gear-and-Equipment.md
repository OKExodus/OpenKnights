# Gear and Equipment

**Definition.** Equipping gear, jewelry, and runes onto a hero's formation slot, and fortifying gear and jewelry with materials or EXP items to raise their level and stats.

## How It Works Inside

A gear or jewelry piece sits in the player's bag or the unequipped jewelry list until assigned to a formation slot's position; assigning it moves the uid between the bag or list and the slot's assignment or block record, and removing it moves the uid back. Runes work the same way in a slot's rune group, with a policy that decides whether a bonus Atk or Def rune rides along when a hero substitution changes who holds the slot, and a combine step that turns runes of one type into the next against the catalog's cost and result.

Fortifying an item takes two forms. A gear or jewelry EXP-item Fortify (C93 / C2641) consumes staged EXP item stacks in request order to raise the target's level toward its cap, recomputing its stats from the item's own catalog curve, and refuses a target already at the cap. A gear Fortify (C81) instead spends material gear pieces, the way a hero Fortify does. Both settle a Gold cost from the catalog before committing.

## Opcodes

| Opcode | Role |
| --- | --- |
| C79 | Equip or unequip gear in a formation slot position |
| C2625 | Equip or unequip jewelry from the unequipped list |
| C1217 / C1219 | Equip or unequip a rune in a slot's rune group |
| C1221 | Combine runes |
| C81 | Gear Fortify (materials) |
| C93 / C2641 | Gear / jewelry EXP-item Fortify |

Equip and rune changes are reached through the formation route; Fortify through `Session.gearFortifyRoute` and `Session.itemFortifyRoute`. See [[Opcode Index]].

## Persistence

Equip, unequip, rune, and Fortify each commit one revision to the character's [[State Store]]. See [[Transactions and Publishing]].

## Determinism

Fortify's bonus multipliers (x1 / x2 / x4 / x10) are never generated by default. Under the labeled local policy `item_fortify_bonus_local_rng_policy_v1`, each consumed item rolls against the policy's odds from a per-transaction seed, recorded so the tallies replay. See [[Method Determinism]].
